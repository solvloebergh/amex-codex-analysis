# Neighborhood median sale price findings

Across 2,930 analyzed sales in 28 neighborhoods, StoneBr has the highest median sale price ($319,000.00; 51 sales), while MeadowV has the lowest ($88,250.00; 37 sales). These are unadjusted neighborhood comparisons. House size, condition, and other factors have not been controlled for, so the differences should not be interpreted as neighborhood effects or causal findings.
